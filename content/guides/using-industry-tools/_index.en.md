{{% notice warning %}}
Tiny Tapeout does not provide any support for those who do not submit using our official tape out flow. It is **up to you**
to ensure that the design passes DRC, precheck and other manufacturability checks. If you fail to do so, your design
**may be rejected or be dead on arrival**.
\
\
Tiny Tapeout does accept designs made via external tools, provided that the GDS & LEF files are valid, and that
the design can be licensed under Apache-2.0.
\
\
**Important**: you must use the open source variants of the target PDK -- SKY130 has both closed and open source variants.
\
\
Resources linked below are community contributions. Tiny Tapeout does not guarantee anything or take any liability if you
follow these guides.
\
\
**We recommend that you use the official tape out flow.**
{{% /notice %}}
