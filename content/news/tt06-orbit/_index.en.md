Lift off!!! 

Our **6th Tiny Tapeout chip** is heading into Low Earth Orbit on Wednesday at 19:20 CET.

For 16 intrepid chip designers, this is an amazing opportunity to test their designs in space!

We love this project because it combines the democratisation of both space and silicon. By aggregating hundreds of designs onto one chip, and then loading hundreds of cubesats on one rocket, testing custom chips in space is possible for just a few hundred dollars! 

[TT06](/chips/tt06) is riding aboard HUNITY, a PocketQube satellite built by Tibor Herman and the team at [BME University](https://www.bme.hu/en) in Hungary. 

If you want a refresher on the project, watch the [interview](/interview-hunity/) we made with Tibor, the creator of the HUNITY satellite we're riding on.

The SpASICs project, led by Pat Deegan, designed the integration and created the board that holds our chip. The satellite will run a series of experiments designed by the community, testing how these designs perform in the radiation environment of Low Earth Orbit.

You can see all the experiments the satellite will run [here](https://github.com/psychogenic/spasic_experiment_testing/tree/main/spasic/experiment).

Join us in the #SpASICs channel of the Tiny Tapeout Discord to watch it live: [tinytapeout.com/discord](/discord)

For up-to-the-minute launch details and the SpaceX livestream, check [nextspaceflight.com](https://nextspaceflight.com/launches/details/7138/). (Weather delays are always a possibility, so stay tuned!)
