[TTGF26a](/chips/ttgf26a/) is open! 

There are 160 tiles on this shuttle, so if you want to tapeout, get your submissions in soon!

The shuttle is filling up fast!

Head over to [app.tinytapeout.com](https://app.tinytapeout.com/) to submit your design.
