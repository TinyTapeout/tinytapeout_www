⚠️ **UPDATE!**

Tiny Tapeout's first online workshop, last July 13 and 17, was a huge success!

{{< figure src="images/matt.jpg" title="Matt's setup" >}}

A total of 59 people attended both sessions: 31.1% were students, 28.9% industry professionals, 22.2% hackers and makers, 13.3% were just curious, and the remainder were professors.

Among the 59 participants, 48 designs were submitted to tapeout. They rated the workshop highly, with an average score of 4.5 out of 5.

> “The explanations were nice and simple and of course the rap songs ;)”

> “I think it's an incredible learning experience that enables students, hobbyists, and professionals to access chip design that would otherwise be impossible!”

> “Honestly can't believe everything is done through Github actions! Impressive!”

> “Tapeout a chip for fun is next level!”

> “The workshop tried to simplify the complex things that make it easy even for a beginner to understand.”
