Demoscene is back! We saw some incredible entries in the last competition and we're looking forward to seeing people push the barriers even further in the silicon demoscene arena.

### What's Different This Year

With 3 different size categories, a new FLASH category, and a newcomer track, there's something for everyone! Whether you're a demoscene veteran or designing your first ASIC, there's a place for you!

### Meet the Judges

We have three hardware legends judging this year.

**[Jeri Ellsworth](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeriellsworth?trk=public_post-text)**

A self-taught chip designer and inventor, Jeri is legendary in the hardware community for squeezing entire systems onto single chips, including a complete Commodore 64 inside a joystick. She pioneered DIY semiconductor fabrication, becoming the first person to demonstrate building a transistor using household items.

**[Will Green](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/wgreen?trk=public_post-text)**

Will runs Project F, an FPGA and hardware education site covering graphics pipelines, VGA timing, framebuffers, and RISC-V. His tutorials have helped countless people bridge the gap from software to FPGAs — making him perfect for judging designs that push display hardware to its limits.

**[Jeroen Domburg (Sprite)](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/jeroen-domburg-63093729?trk=public_post-text)**

A legendary hacker with a gift for making hardware do things it was never meant to. Sprite has ported Doom to a Rigol scope, built a Vectrex multi cart, and created the Hackaday FPGA badge - all with the kind of deranged ingenuity that makes him a natural judge for our ASIC demoscene competition.
