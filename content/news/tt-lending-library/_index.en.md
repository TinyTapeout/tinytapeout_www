Borrow a complete FPGA kit with Pmods and test digital designs on real hardware for 3 months! This is the Tiny Tapeout Lending Library.

It's simple: borrow a kit, test your designs, then pass it on.

### What You Get

An FPGA development kit loaded with everything you need.

- The Tiny Tapeout ETR demoboard with a Lattice UP5K FPGA breakout on top.
- A collection of Pmods:
    - VGA output for graphics. 
    - Rotary encoder.
    - 7-segment display.
    - Audio Pmod. 
    - External flash and RAM.
    - Simon Says game module. 
    - Gamepad Pmod. 
- Gamepad.
- Sticker packs.

### How The Library Works

1️⃣ **Yours for at least 3 months**

The kit is guaranteed to be with you for a minimum of three months. Explore, experiment and have fun!

2️⃣ **Please take care of it**

Handle the boards gently, keep them away from static and liquids, and keep everything together in the bag.

3️⃣ **Be ready to pass it on**

At some point, we will ask you to send the kit on to the next person in the library.

4️⃣ **Keep a sticker pack!**

Feel free to take one of the sticker packs and keep it. That one is yours forever!
