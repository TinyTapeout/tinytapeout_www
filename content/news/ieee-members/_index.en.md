Are you an [IEEE](https://www.ieee.org/) member? 

If you're part of IEEE Division 1, you have free area on our current shuttle [TTSKY26b](/chips/ttsky26b)! Thanks to IEEE Division 1, we have dedicated free tapeout space available right now.

Fill out this form to claim your free coupon: [IEEE Free Tapeout Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd1T44zYhkAqqTiNHxDGq0nVe9D8-h4BbvN0PY-mThRjqDnYw/closedform)

Submission closes on May 18th, 2026. Designs are submitted to the [ChipFoundry](https://chipfoundry.io/) CI2605 shuttle using the [SkyWater](https://www.skywatertechnology.com/) 130nm open source PDK.

For all the details on TTSKY26b, check [tinytapeout.com/chips/ttsky26b/](/chips/ttsky26b/).
