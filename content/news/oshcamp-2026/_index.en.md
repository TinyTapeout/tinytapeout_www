About 80 people showed up to [OSHCamp](https://oshug.org/event/oshcamp2026) in [Hebden Bridge](https://www.hebdenbridge.co.uk/) this past weekend. [Stuart](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pleased/) and [Kris](https://www.linkedin.com/in/krisjdev/) from the Tiny Tapeout team gave two talks.

{{< figure src="images/Stu.jpg" title="Stuart's talk on Chip-on-Board (COB) wire bonding" >}}

Stuart walked through the practicalities of getting bare die connected to PCBs using chip-on-board (COB) wire bonding. Getting custom silicon back from the foundry is amazing, but a piece of silicon won't do much without connectivity. 

He talked about the real-world realities of assembly and the details that matter when you turn up to a factory with an artisanal batch of assorted chips instead of an automated, high-volume run.

{{< figure src="images/Kris.jpg" title="Kris' talk on manufacturing real chips" >}}

Kris then showed what it actually looks like to design and manufacture your own custom chip! He walked through the who, what, how and why of the process, using two of his own designs as examples. 

He also dove into the tools that make it possible  the stuff that turns an idea into silicon you can hold in your hand!

We want to give a huge thanks to everyone who came by! But more importantly, thank you to the OSHCamp team and the [OSHUG](https://oshug.org/) community for hosting!

Till the next one!
