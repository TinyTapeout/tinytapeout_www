Earlier this month, October 1-2, 2026, Tiny Tapeout was part of the two-day IEEE Open Silicon sponsored event held in Panama City: the [LACSS 2026 - the Latin America and Caribbean Semiconductor Summit](https://lacss.ieee.org/)

[Pat Deegan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/patdeegan/) led the workshop. Forty-one participants from Panama, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Brazil, Mexico, El Salvador, and Guatemala all completed their designs which are now heading to fabrication as part of the [TTSKY26d](https://app.tinytapeout.com/shuttles/ttsky26d) shuttle.

It was such a huge success! With an average participant rating of 4.9 out of 5 ⭐️ 

Everybody was engaged and enthusiastic during the entire workshop. Thanks to the local volunteers, who served as TAs for the event, the workshop became an even greater experience for everyone who attended.
