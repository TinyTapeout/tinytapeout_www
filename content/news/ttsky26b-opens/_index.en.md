To our wonderful surprise, [TTSKY26a](/chips/ttsky26a) sold out as fast as the previous shuttle! 

Due to the high demand, we've opened [TTSKY26b](/chips/ttsky26b), taping out on the 11th of May.

This shuttle is supported by the [IEEE](https://www.ieee.org/). Half the area and PCBs have been reserved for them, but there's still plenty of space if you want to get involved!

40 non-subsidized PCBs are available for anyone to submit designs.

Head over to [app.tinytapeout.com](https://app.tinytapeout.com/) to get your design in before the deadline.
