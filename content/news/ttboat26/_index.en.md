We've seen so many amazing designs from the Tiny Tapeout community in the past couple of years. Some teach, some surprise, some inspire, and some have become favourites that people keep coming back to. 

So we asked a simple question: **what would the best designs of all time look like if we put them together? 🤔**

<img src="images/epic.gif" alt="epic" style="display:block; margin-right:auto; margin-left:0; max-width:100%;">

Yup, pretty epic.

In partnership with [wafer.space](https://wafer.space/), we are making a curated chip of the best Tiny Tapeout projects from across the community on one shared chip! 

The Tiny Tapeout BOAT will be available as an **add-on** when you tapeout. It will be available from mid 2027.
 
### The BOAT

The BOAT will include:
- [Mini MOSbius](/news/mini-mosbius/), an analog FPGA for learning analog microelectronics
- A [tiny FPGA](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_fabulous_ihp_26b) from Leo Moser and the FABulous team
- Some of the winners of the different [competitions](/competitions) we've had, like the [TinyQV RISCV](/chips/tt06/tt_um_MichaelBell_tinyQV) by Mike Bell, and [Glyph Mode](/chips/tt08/tt_um_vga_glyph_mode) by James Ross
- Chip of the Week selections
- And many more!

Open source is at the heart of what we do and sharing these designs isn't just about making them accessible online. We want to share them physically too! That way, not only can they be in the hands of the people who designed them, but also in the hands of the people who want to learn from them.

### What design do you want on board?

You get a chance to participate and choose a design that deserves a place on the BOAT! If you have a favourite you want to see included, even if it's your own, let us know at [tiny.tt/boat](http://tiny.tt/boat).
