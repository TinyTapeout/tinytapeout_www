Switzerland has an incredible engineering community, so we'd love to see more Swiss designs on silicon.

**Free Webinar: March 10th**

On Tuesday, March 10th at 18:00 CET we're running a free 1-hour online webinar to get you started with chip design. No prior experience required.

In the session, we'll cover:

- How to build a simple digital design
- The submission process step-by-step
- How to claim your SwissChips coupon code
- How Tiny Tapeout works
- What happens after tapeout

### Free Chips for Swiss Residents

Thanks to support from [SwissChips](https://swisschips.ethz.ch/), participants based in Switzerland can receive a coupon code for a free submission on our upcoming [TTIHP26a](/chips/ttihp26a/) shuttle, as well as a free chip mounted on a PCB development kit. Every chip will include hundreds of designs from the community that submitted to the shuttle, and you can try them all out!

Following the webinar you'll have time to build a small project, register and get your coupon, and submit your design.

### Sign Up

Coupons for free submissions, chips, and PCBs are limited, so sign up early! Register for the webinar [here](https://tinytapeout.com/swisschips2026).
