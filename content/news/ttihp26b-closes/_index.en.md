That's wrap! We closed [TTIHP26b](/chips/ttihp26b/) yesterday. 123 projects submitted to the IHP-2609 shuttle, heading to manufacturing on the 0.13μm process.

Chips are expected on June 25, 2027, with estimated shipping on August 16, 2027.

Here are some of the designs:

Leo and the Fabulous team built a full FPGA on a single Tiny Tapeout tile, the [Tiny FABulous FPGA](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_fabulous_ihp_26b). Then we have [Preinception](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_preinception_top), a simple compute accelerator, connected via USB and driven with a C++ API and code on the host PC. There's also a [Scalable Banked L1 Memory Fabric](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_vperumal_l1_fabric) purpose-built for edge AI.

On the analog side, we've got [IEEE Bandgap References](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_chip_ieee_analog), [LDO regulators](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_ieee_LDO), [SAR ADC controllers](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_josue_olivos_sar_adc), and a [laser gyro readout core!](/chips/ttihp26b/tt_um_gyro_lockin).
