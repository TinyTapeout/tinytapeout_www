**Tiny Tapeout** was at the [ORConf 2026](https://fossi-foundation.org/orconf/2026) last week, from September 11-13. Matt Venn gave a talk updating the community about the latest news and our plans for the future.

### 🏆 Our Recent Wins

Matt started his update by sharing the exciting news of us **launching chips into [space!](/news/tt06-orbit/)** Everyone with a design on [TT06](/chips/tt06) got to run it in low orbit. Matt's ring oscillator showed temperature fluctuations as the satellite rotated in and out of the sun's shadow. That's real silicon doing real work 400km up!

Another cool thing we did was **[a chip surgery](/news/chip-surgery/) with a FIB machine** — cutting and rejoining microscopic wires inside actual silicon. 

Matt then shared Tiny Tapeout's recognition at the **[European Open Source Award](/news/matt-wins-award/)** from David Guatiellis, one of the Arduino founders
{{< figure src="images/award.jpg" title="New Tiny Tapeout Explorer" >}}

And finally, Matt talked about our **[RISC-V competition](/competitions/risc-v-peripheral-winners/)**, which was a huge success, pulling in 44 entries. It was the biggest Tiny Tapeout design so far at 60,000 standard cells! There were a lot of interesting and crazy projects in it, one of which won the award 'most unusual'. The [DWARF Line Table Accelerator](https://github.com/TinyTapeout/ttsky25a-tinyQV/blob/main/docs/user_peripherals/34_dwarf_line_table_accelerator.md) by Laurie Hedge.

### 📈 Tools and Growth

**The Tiny Tapeout community is growing!** The latest community stats have been very exciting and also heartwarming to see. 5,000 members on [Discord](https://tinytapeout.com/discord). 4,000 projects manufactured to date, and 24 chips completed! We are on track for 3,000 projects this year across 10 shuttles. Next year we're expecting 4,000 projects across 17 shuttles.

One notable contribution from our community is the **new 3D viewer (Tiny Tapeout Explorer)**.  It doesn't just show your design in 3D, but it also extracts the netlist and simulates it running in the browser. So you can see your chip working before it even gets manufactured. Pretty neat!

{{< figure src="images/Explorer.jpg" title="New Tiny Tapeout Explorer" >}}

### ⏭️ What's Coming

**The Tiny Tapeout GOAT!** We're ordering 1,000 designs from wafer.space and making a curated chip of the best Tiny Tapeout projects. This includes Mini Mosbius (an analog FPGA for learning analog microelectronics),  a [tiny FPGA](/chips/ttihp26a/tt_um_fabulous_ihp_26a) from Leo and the Fabulous team, and all the winners of the different [competitions](/competitions/) we've had.
