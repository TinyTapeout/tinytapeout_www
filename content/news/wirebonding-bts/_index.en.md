A lot of you have asked about our lovely chip-on-board wirebonding. 

Here's how it actually works!

### The Process

The COB (chip-on-board) is loaded into a jig, and the wirebonding machine is calibrated for the chip pads. The die is carefully positioned and glued to the PCB, forming the chip-on-board.

The wirebonder then connects hundreds of microscopic wires from the chip to the PCB traces. And here's the impressive part: the machine can do the whole chip in one go, automatically connecting every pad without human intervention.

Once the wirebonding is complete, a clear epoxy is applied to protect the die from damage and contamination.

### The Result

The finished boards are ready for testing. From bare die to working chip-on-board, it's a meticulous process that turns silicon into something you can actually use.
